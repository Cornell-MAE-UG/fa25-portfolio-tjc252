## Fluid-Mechanical Dissection

For this project, I will select a real-world device, take it apart, and explain the fluid mechanics that make it work. The final deliverable will include video content walking through the device's internal flow paths and the governing physics behind its operation.

---

## Planned Content

- **Device overview:** what the device is, what it does, and why it was chosen
- **Dissection:** documented teardown of the device's fluid-handling components
- **Fluid-mechanical analysis:** the conservation laws, pressure and velocity fields, and flow behavior that govern its performance
- **Video explanation:** a narrated walkthrough connecting the hardware to the underlying fluid mechanics
